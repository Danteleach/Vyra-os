# Vyra Branding — Phase 1 tokens

Source: Master Directive §12. Text wordmark only in Phase 1.

**Do not invent a logo.** Official VFM logo assets were not on disk at the
2026-11-20 audit (only a favicon monogram and colour tokens existed).
If official logo files are supplied, replace the wordmark with them and
record the source file, date, and licence here. Until then, use the text
`Vyra` / `Vyra OS` in the display face below — no generic logo, no
copied distribution logo.
